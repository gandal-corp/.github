build mobile apps, streaming platforms and payment integrations.

  Android TV and Apple TV. *Coming soon.*
- **Actu Guinée**: real-time news on iOS and Android. *Coming soon.*
- **Orange Money for Shopify**: lets Shopify merchants in Guinea accept Orange
  Money. *Coming soon.*
